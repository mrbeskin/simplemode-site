who we are

Most teams don’t need more technology. They need the right technology.

Software projects usually fail for a simple reason: they get overcomplicated. Vendors oversell, scope balloons, timelines slip. We started simplemode to do the opposite. We take the time to understand your problem, build the simplest thing that solves it well, and hand you something your team can actually live with.

simplemode is deliberately small — no account managers, no handoffs to a junior bench. The people below are the people you’ll work with: senior engineers whose paths barely overlap, from platform architecture at Microsoft to devices and robotics at Amazon to AI in healthcare. Between us, most problems look familiar from somewhere.

Michael Beskin

cofounder

Michael has spent twelve years building systems that have to work every time: client code in Alexa’s speech-recognition path, hardware abstraction layers for ATMs — where a bug isn’t an inconvenience, it’s someone’s money — deployment systems for Amazon’s Scout delivery robots already out in the world, and the evaluation harness for Amazon’s foundation models.
